Filing-Evidence

Can small open models read SEC filings with evidence a machine can check? Ten models, 1,000 questions, every answer published.

What we found

01

Small open models can do much of this job.

In the plain setup accuracy ranges from 17.1% to 82.7%. Qwen3.5-9B is ahead of every other model by a statistically clear margin.

02

A checkable quote helps, but isn't enough.

The checker stops most wrong answers. Yet the answers that pass every check include 2.4–31.8% wrong ones: correct-looking evidence, wrong number.

03

Those errors are specific and fixable.

They are almost all units read wrongly or the neighbouring column (another year or quarter) of the right line, not invented numbers.

04

Thinking helps more than retrying.

Thinking cut wrong-but-passing answers for every model that has the mode, at 8–67 times the generation cost. Retrying after a failed check never reduced them.
