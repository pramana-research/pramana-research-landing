Vision

Trust is earned one verified answer at a time.

General-purpose models are fluent, but in high-stakes work fluency is not enough. Professionals need models that know their domain, read the documents in front of them, and leave a trail a reviewer can follow.

We take the best open-weight models, specialise them one domain at a time on data whose correctness we can prove, and release them only when they beat what came before. Because they are open-weight, organisations can run them on their own infrastructure, with their own data.

01

Verified sources

Training data comes from authoritative records, so every answer is correct by construction and cites where it came from.

02

Show the work

Models read the source instead of recalling from memory, and show the reasoning behind each answer so it can be checked in seconds.

03

Honest evaluation

Measured on cases the model has never seen, reported by task, with plain statements of what a result does not prove.

04

Yours to run

Open-weight models deploy on your own servers, so sensitive data never has to leave your organisation.

Gananka: the groundwork of equity research, checked.

from Sanskrit gaṇanā, calculation, and gaṇaka, the one who calculates

Gananka reads what public companies reported to the SEC and does the extraction and arithmetic analysts do every day, citing the figures it used. It is a tool for analysts, not a stock picker: no buy or sell calls, no price targets.

Results on companies it had never seen

784 questions on 10 companies held out entirely from training. Every answer is checked against the figures the company filed.
