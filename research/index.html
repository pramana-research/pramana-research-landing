Community research report · Not peer-reviewed · 17 November 2026 · Krishna Nandula

Show Your Work: Can Small Open Models Read SEC Filings with Evidence a Machine Can Check?

Ten open models on one consumer GPU, 1,000 questions over 90 SEC filings. The best answers 84.5% correctly with a quote a machine can check; even so, wrong answers that pass every check remain, and we show exactly what they are.
