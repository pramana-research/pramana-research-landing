Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 584 answers were correct and 77 were wrong while passing every check: 53 scale or unit; 16 another period or column of the right line; 8 a different item. A further 104 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 31 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on main statements, current period (92.8%) and 10-Q quarter vs year-to-date (77.6%); weakest on numbers in prose (40.8%) and units and per-share (52.8%).

What stands out

  • Among the best at saying "not found" (124 of 125).
  • No reasoning mode. Retry adds 2.9 points of accuracy but also 9 confident-wrong answers.
