Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 537 answers were correct and 117 were wrong while passing every check: 64 scale or unit; 29 another period or column of the right line; 24 a different item. A further 121 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 88 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on main statements, current period (81.6%) and main statements, prior period (80.0%); weakest on numbers in prose (40.8%) and units and per-share (44.0%).

Check + retry asked 214 answers again. Of those, 26 became correct, 17 became confident-wrong, 36 turned into "not found", and the rest still failed a check.

What stands out

  • The weakest of the ten at saying "not found" (97 of 125): it most often answers a quarterly figure asked of an annual report, where the correct answer is "not found" (17 of 31 correct).
  • Run with FP8 weights; retry raised its confident-wrong answers from 117 to 134.
