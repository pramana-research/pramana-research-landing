Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 724 answers were correct and 36 were wrong while passing every check: 2 scale or unit; 25 another period or column of the right line; 9 a different item. A further 46 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 4 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on 10-Q quarter vs year-to-date (96.0%) and main statements, current period (94.4%); weakest on numbers in prose (57.6%) and segments (71.2%).

Check + retry asked 50 answers again. Of those, 15 became correct, 0 became confident-wrong, 9 turned into "not found", and the rest still failed a check.

What stands out

  • The most accurate model in the plain and check + retry setups, and significantly ahead of every other model in both.
  • The fewest confident-wrong answers in every setup it was compared in; with thinking, 16 in 1,000, the lowest in the study.
  • Thinking lowered its accuracy to 74.6% because it often reasoned past the pre-registered 8,192-token limit: 180 truncated answers, 88 of them unparseable. On the questions it finished, accuracy was 85.1%.
