Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 515 answers were correct and 102 were wrong while passing every check: 62 scale or unit; 23 another period or column of the right line; 17 a different item. A further 128 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 101 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on 10-Q quarter vs year-to-date (85.6%) and main statements, prior period (71.2%); weakest on segments (32.8%) and numbers in prose (40.8%).

What stands out

  • The oldest model in the study (released 2024-07), statistically level with Gemma-3-12B and Phi-4.
