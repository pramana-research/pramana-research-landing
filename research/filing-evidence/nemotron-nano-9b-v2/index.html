Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 370 answers were correct and 105 were wrong while passing every check: 66 scale or unit; 28 another period or column of the right line; 11 a different item. A further 159 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 67 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on main statements, current period (62.4%) and 10-Q quarter vs year-to-date (56.0%); weakest on numbers in prose (26.4%) and units and per-share (26.4%).

Check + retry asked 226 answers again. Of those, 1 became correct, 0 became confident-wrong, 198 turned into "not found", and the rest still failed a check.

What stands out

  • When told that a check failed, it almost always gives up: of 226 retried answers, 198 became "not found". Retry therefore changes accuracy by only +0.1 points.
  • Thinking is where it improves most: +12.1 points of accuracy, from 42.3% to 54.4%.
  • It never invented an answer for a question whose answer was not on the pages in the plain setup (125 of 125).
