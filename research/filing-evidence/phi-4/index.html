Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 501 answers were correct and 151 were wrong while passing every check: 116 scale or unit; 17 another period or column of the right line; 18 a different item. A further 107 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 69 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on main statements, current period (72.0%) and 10-Q quarter vs year-to-date (70.4%); weakest on segments (29.6%) and numbers in prose (47.2%).

Check + retry asked 177 answers again. Of those, 31 became correct, 16 became confident-wrong, 12 turned into "not found", and the rest still failed a check.

What stands out

  • The most confident-wrong answers of the ten (151 in the plain setup), and 116 of them are scale or unit errors: right line, right digits, but "scale: none" for a table in millions. Ignoring the stated scale its accuracy would be 70.5%.
  • Retry makes this worse: 16 answers went from caught to confident-wrong after the retry.
  • Run with FP8 weights (14.7B parameters do not fit in 24 GB in bf16).
