Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 641 answers were correct and 64 were wrong while passing every check: 16 scale or unit; 35 another period or column of the right line; 13 a different item. A further 48 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 69 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on main statements, current period (96.0%) and notes tables (86.4%); weakest on numbers in prose (41.6%) and segments (55.2%).

Check + retry asked 122 answers again. Of those, 26 became correct, 8 became confident-wrong, 0 turned into "not found", and the rest still failed a check.

What stands out

  • Second-fewest confident-wrong answers among the ten in the plain setup (64), and 38 with thinking.
  • Thinking raises accuracy to 81.3%, statistically level with the best thinking-mode results, at about 52 times the tokens.
  • It thinks by default; the plain setup switches thinking off through its chat template.
