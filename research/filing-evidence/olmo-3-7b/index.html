Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 150 answers were correct and 70 were wrong while passing every check: 37 scale or unit; 29 another period or column of the right line; 4 a different item. A further 231 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 299 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on main statements, current period (34.4%) and notes tables (20.8%); weakest on numbers in prose (8.0%) and main statements, prior period (9.6%).

Check + retry asked 537 answers again. Of those, 1 became correct, 0 became confident-wrong, 530 turned into "not found", and the rest still failed a check.

What stands out

  • It usually finds the right number but rarely quotes the line exactly: it inserts dollar signs the page doesn't have ("26,873 $ 24,248"), repeats figures or joins lines. 231 answers had the right value with a quote the checker could not accept.
  • Retry hardly helps (17.3%): asked again, it repeats the same kind of quote.
