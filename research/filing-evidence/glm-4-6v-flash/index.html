Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 620 answers were correct and 110 were wrong while passing every check: 67 scale or unit; 35 another period or column of the right line; 8 a different item. A further 45 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 8 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on main statements, current period (92.0%) and 10-Q quarter vs year-to-date (86.4%); weakest on numbers in prose (37.6%) and units and per-share (57.6%).

Check + retry asked 124 answers again. Of those, 19 became correct, 1 became confident-wrong, 1 turned into "not found", and the rest still failed a check.

What stands out

  • The largest gain from thinking in the study (+11.4 points, to 82.3%, the highest thinking-mode accuracy), with short reasoning: 385 tokens per answer on average.
  • Thinking also cut its confident-wrong answers from 110 to 61.
  • A vision-language model, run text-only (the vision encoder was not loaded).
