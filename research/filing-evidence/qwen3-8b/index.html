Where the answers go

In the plain setup, 578 answers were correct and 94 were wrong while passing every check: 41 scale or unit; 37 another period or column of the right line; 16 a different item. A further 100 had the right value with evidence the checker could not accept, and 88 wrong answers were rejected by the checker.

By kind of content

Plain setup: strongest on main statements, current period (89.6%) and main statements, prior period (84.0%); weakest on numbers in prose (33.6%) and units and per-share (46.4%).

What stands out

  • The model of the pilot study that motivated the benchmark, reported as a supplementary baseline outside the ten-model comparison (Qwen3.5-9B is the Alibaba entry).
  • Weak at "not found" in the plain setup (88 of 125): asked for a quarter of an annual report, it answered "not found" only 6 times in 31.
  • Thinking brings it to 74.1% and cuts confident-wrong answers from 94 to 37.
